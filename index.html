Phone ki battery zyada der kaise chalayein

31 October 2026

Screen ki brightness auto par rakhein, jin apps ki zaroorat nahi unki background activity band karein, aur raat ko phone poora 0 tak na utarein. Ye teen cheezein battery ki zindagi mein sab se zyada farq daalti hain.

Agar phone phir bhi jaldi khatam ho jaye to Settings mein Battery section dekhein, wahan wo app mil jaye gi jo sab se zyada battery kha rahi hai.

Slow internet ko tez karne ke 5 tareeqay

30 October 2026

Router ko khuli jagah rakhein, purane devices jo WiFi se jude hain unhein hata dein, aur router ko hafte mein ek baar restart karein. Browser ka cache saaf karna bhi page loading tez kar deta hai.

Agar masla rahe to apne internet provider se speed test ke saath rabta karein.

Online scam se kaise bachein

29 October 2026

Kisi ko apna OTP ya password kabhi na batayein, chahe wo bank ka naam le. Anjaan links par click karne se pehle address dhyan se parhein, aur jo offer bohat zyada achi lage us par shak karein.
